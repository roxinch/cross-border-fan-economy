# Cross-Border Fan-Economy Platform — Case Study

A public case study of leading the proposal-to-launch phase of a cross-border APAC fan-economy venture (Q1–Q2 2026), including 3-year P&L architecture, 5-module business model, and treasury and compliance framework.

**Live:** https://roxinch.github.io/cross-border-fan-economy/

## About

This single-page case study documents the design decisions behind a multi-module fan-economy platform spanning live events, e-commerce, member subscriptions, and cross-currency payment infrastructure across multiple APAC jurisdictions.

**Key outcomes**

- $200M+ projected 3-year aggregate GMV across all modules
- 5 business modules architected and tied into one P&L
- 53–90% margin range across modules
- Multi-currency settlement design (4+ currencies)
- Two-mode capital injection structure with milestone gating
- Four-stage transaction lifecycle (order → payment → fulfillment → reconciliation)
- All investor sign-off milestones met on schedule
- Operating team handover completed at launch

**Architectural pillars**

- Five-module ecosystem (live events, member platform, e-commerce + retail, payment infrastructure, IP licensing as Phase 2)
- Capital governance with milestone-gated tranches and budget caps
- Treasury hierarchy with escrow-backed sub-accounts and audit-ready reconciliation
- Multi-stakeholder decision-rights matrix (investor / operations / payment partner / regional partners)
